# Handoff Instructions

A handoff changes workflow ownership without losing relationship context.

Required handoff data:

`CONTACT | COMPANY | FROM | TO | RELATIONSHIP | CURRENT STATUS | CONTEXT | LAST ACTION | USER APPROVAL | NEXT ACTION | PRIORITY | DEADLINE | SOURCE | NOTES`

## Rules

- never hand off only a name;
- preserve original targeting reason;
- preserve commitments and open questions;
- preserve approval scope;
- do not seize an already-owned active relationship without explicit reason;
- update owner in CRM when ownership genuinely changes.
