# LinkedIn Account Safety

Protect the account above throughput.

Never:

- bypass CAPTCHA, identity verification, rate limits, security challenges, or access controls;
- imitate human timing to avoid detection;
- rotate accounts, sessions, proxies, identities, or devices to evade restrictions;
- use unauthorized scraping/automation extensions as a workaround;
- mass-send identical or near-identical DMs, invitations, comments, or reactions;
- keep retrying an action after LinkedIn warns or restricts the account.

If LinkedIn shows CAPTCHA, verification, unusual activity, an invitation/messaging restriction, a temporary limitation, or another security notice, stop the affected workflow, record what happened, and require human review.

Optimize for relevance and genuine professional context, not maximum action volume.
