# Approval Policy

## Read actions
No approval required for reading inboxes, profiles, posts, connection status, public company information, or CRM records; classifying, summarizing, researching, and drafting.

## Preparation actions
No approval required for preparing a DM, reply, follow-up, connection note, comment, post, shortlist, task, or CRM update proposal.

## External LinkedIn actions
Approval required by default for:

- sending a DM or reply;
- sending, accepting, declining, or withdrawing a connection request;
- commenting or reacting;
- publishing;
- editing profile/Page content;
- any other action visible to another person.

Do not turn one approval into standing permission.

## Always escalate
Escalate pricing negotiations, contractual/legal commitments, complaints, public disputes, hiring offers, partnership commitments, financial promises, sensitive client matters, or other reputationally sensitive actions.

## Approval record
For approved external actions record the person, action, content/draft, approval state, approval scope, date/timestamp when available, and outcome.
