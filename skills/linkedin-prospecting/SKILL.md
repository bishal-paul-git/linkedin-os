# LinkedIn Prospecting & Network

Read the shared policies in `../_shared/` before execution.

## Scope

Own new-person discovery, qualification, connection strategy, and handoff after acceptance. Do not own ongoing DM conversations once a relationship becomes active.

## Intake

Use explicit:

- target industry
- company list or company type
- ICP/persona
- role/seniority
- geography
- named person
- partner type
- problem/use case
- result limit

Do not silently broaden hard filters.

## Workflow

1. Read user configuration and Run Control.
2. Search LinkedIn or available people sources.
3. Evaluate current role, company, seniority, functional relevance, industry, location, company fit, reason the relationship matters, and evidence.
4. Check CRM/LinkedIn state for existing connection, past conversation, client/partner status, exclusions, do-not-contact, or current owner.
5. Reject duplicates and weak fits.
6. Group candidates as Strong Fit, Good Fit, Possible, or Rejected.
7. State a genuine reason to connect and next action.
8. Prepare a connection note only when it adds material context.
9. Require approval before connection actions.
10. Record approved actions and hand accepted connections to Inbox/CRM with original targeting context.

## Connection states

`DISCOVERED | QUALIFIED | APPROVED | CONNECTION PREPARED | INVITED | ACCEPTED | CONVERSATION STARTED | ACTIVE | OPPORTUNITY | NOT INTERESTED | DORMANT | DO NOT CONTACT`

Never reset an existing relationship to a new-prospect state.

## Output

For recommended people include:

- Name
- Role
- Company
- Location when useful
- Why Fit
- Relationship/Opportunity Rationale
- Connection Status
- CRM History
- Recommended Action
- Profile URL/source

Keep rejected and uncertain candidates separate.
